# Changelog (Preview)

Versions of the Preview channel. Once a version becomes the normal HomeAce Studio, its full notes are in
the normal changelog.

## 0.14.2-preview.2

- The "Preview" mark in the top bar opens a short note: what the Preview is, what carries over to the
  normal app, and how to give feedback. A dot shows until you have opened it.
- Choosing a device or entity: after picking an area, the area list closes and the matches show at
  once. Long area lists scroll, and the window keeps its size.
- On narrow phones the top bar fits the screen again; below 420 px the name gives way, the logo stays.
- Link buttons such as "Give feedback" are no longer underlined.

## 0.14.2-preview.1

- Tidied up inside: clearer code under the hood, ready for the next features. Everything works and
  looks as in 0.14.1.
- New in the Preview: a small "Preview" mark in the top bar and "Give feedback" under About HomeAce.
